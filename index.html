Practical steps

Replace lost ID

  1. Write down which documents are missing. You do not need to store their numbers here. If theft or identity fraud is involved, review the official identity-theft guidance.
  2. Choose your province or territory in Resources. Ask its income-support office or a community worker about ID-replacement help, fees and an address for receiving mail.
  3. For a birth certificate, apply through the province or territory where you were born. For a health card or photo ID, use your current province or territory’s official service office. Ask what alternate proof is accepted if you have no ID.
  4. For a Social Insurance Number, use Service Canada’s official process. Keep originals and application receipts safe; do not send identity documents to this site.

Daily needs and a place out of the weather

Ask your local service navigator or municipality about warming or cooling centres, showers, laundry, public washrooms, charging, mail pickup and storage. Confirm opening hours, accessibility, fees and what you can bring.

A library or drop-in may have free Wi-Fi, computers or a phone. Avoid recording sensitive details on a public computer. Do not assume Wi-Fi alone can send SMS texts.

Keep your housing

If rent is overdue or you receive an eviction notice, seek local legal advice promptly. Choose your region’s legal-aid service and ask a housing worker about rent banks, emergency benefits, utility help and eviction-prevention programs. Rules and deadlines vary by province and territory.

About and privacy

Who maintains this tool

An independent, free community resource project maintained through Khayoon’s public GitHub repositories. It is not a government agency, shelter, counselling service or live service navigator.

Project and maintainer (opens a new tab)

What stays on this device

Journal entries, goals and reminders are handled in this browser. No account or analytics are used. Choose session-only use on a shared device, or protect saved personal data with a passphrase in My data. Without that protection, anyone using this browser profile may read saved personal data. Device malware or someone with an unlocked session can still read what is on screen.

This site uses system fonts and makes no third-party font or tracking requests. The host still receives normal connection information, such as an IP address. Opening another service’s website, sending a text, making a call or submitting a GitHub report shares information with that provider under its own privacy rules.

Quick exit hides this page and opens a neutral website. It does not erase browser history, downloads, saved data or monitoring records. Use My data to delete this app’s personal records. Exported and printed copies need their own protection.

Offline access and saved data

Offline setup has not been confirmed. An internet connection is needed for the first visit.

Once this browser has saved the app for offline use, its directory and personal tools can reopen without internet. Service websites and chat still need internet; calls and SMS need a working telephone service. Browsers can remove stored data. Installing may improve retention but is not a backup. Export what you need and keep the file safe.

Report a wrong listing

Each listing shows when its official page was checked. This does not mean the phone line was called or a place is available. Report the listing name and public correction; do not include your journal, ID, location or someone else’s private information. GitHub reports are public and require an account. You can also report incorrect service information directly to its provider.
