# Directory source checks

Last source check: 2026-11-05 (America/Toronto).

This is a directory of service providers and official information, not a live bed inventory, availability guarantee, eligibility decision, medical advice or legal advice. “Page checked” means the linked provider/government page was reviewed through web research; no phone calls or applications were made. Source pages can change. Check the provider before travel. The English and French summaries are editorial translations; an external website may not offer both languages.

## Coverage and caveats

- 66 entries: 22 Canada-wide information/locators, income, legal and health entries for all 13 provinces and territories, and 5 explicitly Toronto-only shelter/daily-needs records. National entries may refer to regional programs rather than offer one identical service throughout Canada.
- 211's official page says “in most of Canada.” Phone, text, chat and hours vary by regional provider. The directory links to the provider selector; daily-needs guidance describes what to ask/search for, not an assertion that each community has those services.
- Health numbers are region-specific. Manitoba's current provider publishes 1-888-315-9257; no unverified 811 assumption is made. Nunavut uses community health-centre contacts rather than an invented territorial 811 line. The Government of Nunavut facilities page rendered without local contacts in the research tool, so the Department of Health's Nunavut Nurses contact directory is supplied instead.
- Quebec's current Info-Santé page explicitly excludes Nunavik and the Cree Territory of James Bay. The summary preserves that source's caveat; local services should be consulted in those regions.
- Legal aid is not a promise of a free lawyer or that housing/benefits issues are covered. Financial eligibility, covered issues and contributions vary. Justice Canada's national list was used for discovery, then provider pages were checked. Its obsolete CSJ .aspx and nulas.ca links were replaced with current provider URLs.
- ShelterSafe serves women and children experiencing violence. It is not a general shelter-bed booking service and no available bed is promised.
- Kids Help Phone's official pages support CONNECT in English and PARLER in French. Online crisis messaging is distinct from professional live counselling chat, whose hours vary; no universal live-chat-hours promise is made. The source's page has contradictory live-chat-hours widgets, so only a direction to check current hours is given.
- Hope for Wellness serves Indigenous people across Canada; languages should be checked with the service. No claim of unlimited confidentiality or no emergency intervention is made for any helpline.
- CMHC's renting guide pages are longstanding general information. The summaries direct people to current local rental authorities/legal clinics and do not reproduce dated COVID rules or claim one national eviction deadline.
- Federal Benefits Finder now describes federal programs. It is not represented as a complete source of provincial benefits.
- Free tax clinic eligibility is limited to modest income and simple tax situations. Naloxone access, cost and eligibility vary by province/territory.
- Public libraries, community centres and drop-ins may provide Wi-Fi, computers or phones. Users are instructed to ask locally; universal access is not promised.
- ShelterSafe warns 911 is unavailable in Nunavut. The app's emergency panel should acknowledge regional availability and direct people to local emergency services; no universal 911 claim should be made.

## Additional verification pages

### Toronto local records

- Toronto Central Intake is explicitly local and the official page confirms its toll-free number and 24/7 referral service. A suitable shelter place is not guaranteed.
- Warming-centre records link to the official activation/location page without freezing a weather threshold, address list or live opening status into the offline directory.
- Toronto's cooling map is seasonal; its page says the map is not maintained outside heat season. The summary instructs users to check current seasonal status, hours and access.
- Drop-in services vary by location and the City specifically advises calling ahead. No universal shower/laundry availability is promised.
- The public washroom map supplies accessibility/status information; local opening hours still require checking.
